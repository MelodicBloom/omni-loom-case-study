## Repository-specific records

### D20 — Omni-Loom: craft instrument and artifact-authorized fabrication architecture

**Use when:** When recovering Omni-Loom's original interface, compiler, physics, generators, AI tools, worker architecture, or case study.

[Recovered intent] The three-part design spec is much broader than an architecture diagram. It describes Dashboard, Workbench, and Forge; a queue rail, viewscreen, compiler visualization, physics/material regimes, procedural pattern generator, AI playground/refinery, camera overlay, GCode inspection, and tactile preview. Warm Loom, Brutalist Forge, and immersive glass profiles express different tasks.

[Behavior] Preserve the Behavioral Trinity as a proposed coupling of physics, rendering, and chosen audio/haptic cues. Shytech keeps expensive/sensitive capabilities dormant until needed. The digital-to-physical route should preserve intent and produce inspectable artifacts, manifests, and receipts before transport.

[Observed boundaries] The current case-study README explicitly calls its browser demo simulated and physical dispatch disabled. The comprehensive briefing's enterprise/certification/latency/first-of-kind language lacks supporting proof in this pass. The original parts propose Astro/Svelte/R3F, Node/Fastify, Python services, Rust/WASM, and workers; the later briefing describes a different backend. These are contexts/options to reconcile, not one accepted stack.

[Repair resolution — proposed] Preserve UX ambition while separating planner, deterministic geometry/compiler, validator, artifact store, authorization, and execution-only worker. Do not equate structured AI output with manufacturability. Replace universal kerf numbers with dated machine/material/process profiles. A runtime frame miss should not automatically invalidate unrelated physical job state; define recovery per domain.

[Reimagined horizon] A legible craft instrument where people can compose, simulate, compare, inspect, and eventually fabricate with real process qualification. Start with a local dry-run that remains useful without cloud orchestration. Real hardware is a separate authorized lane.

[Carry-forward test] One source geometry/job yields repeatable dry-run outputs, preview, manifest, gate report, and clear rejection behavior. Later physical qualification adds machine/profile/measurement evidence without rewriting authored intent.

## Practical continuation contract

1. Re-read current repository instructions and source before implementation. The baseline above identifies this publication, not a permanent execution HEAD.
2. Retain the full design horizon separately from the current demonstrator. Name what the demonstrator preserves, approximates, defers, or cannot prove.
3. Map tokens and controls to actual consumers with explicit units, ownership, accessible alternatives, and observable effect.
4. Complete the relevant interaction/error/cancellation states before promoting a reusable component.
5. Pair source/runtime checks with human correspondence review against the actual visual inspiration.
6. Keep proposed repairs unexecuted until a later implementation records its own revision, checks, and evidence.
